Questions

Good to know.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Pagerbit runs entirely on your Mac, reads your local Claude Code transcripts, and talks only to a backend bound to 127.0.0.1. No account, no sign-in, no telemetry. Your API keys and tokens are encrypted on-device.

Which agents and terminals are supported?

Claude Code and Codex CLI today, with more agents on the roadmap. Click a session and Pagerbit switches to the exact tab running it in Terminal or iTerm2; Warp, Ghostty, WezTerm, kitty, Hyper, Alacritty and Tabby are brought to the front.

How do approvals work?

A tiny hook — installed by the first-run wizard, with your settings.json backed up first — forwards Claude Code permission requests to the notch. If Pagerbit isn't running, your normal terminal prompt takes over: it can never block or silently allow anything.

Was this TokenLens?

Yes. TokenLens is now Pagerbit — same app, same license, same data and the same update channel. Existing buyers get the new name automatically.

What does the trial include?

Everything, for 14 days, no card. After that the app keeps running; you'll just see a gentle reminder to activate. No features are ripped out from under you.

Can I move my license to a new Mac?

Yes. Deactivate in Settings ▸ License on the old Mac and activate on the new one. A 1-Mac license means one Mac at a time.

What do I get during pre-sale?

The current build plus a full year of updates at the $15 price — the roadmap (more agents, richer styles, an iOS companion) lands as free updates during that year.
