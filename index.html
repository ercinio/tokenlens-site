Questions

Good to know

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. TokenLens runs entirely on your Mac, reads your local Claude Code transcripts, and talks only to a backend bound to 127.0.0.1. No account, no sign-in, no telemetry. Your API keys and tokens are encrypted on-device.

Which agents and terminals are supported?

Claude Code and Codex CLI today, with more agents on the roadmap. Click a session and TokenLens switches to the exact tab running it in Terminal or iTerm2; Warp, Ghostty, WezTerm, kitty, Hyper, Alacritty and Tabby are brought to the front.

What's new in the latest version?

0.9.1 (Sep 13, 2026): computer use cleans up after itself — the driver daemon exits with the app however it is closed, leftovers from earlier copies are ended on launch, and quitting from the terminal is now an orderly quit. 0.9.0 (Sep 13, 2026): local models with one button — the Local page names what is installed (Ollama, LM Studio), fetches what is not, and sets capture up in one call; the local proxy is now its own launchd agent, so quitting TokenLens no longer takes local models offline or leaves a hole in the record; when nothing is being counted the page says why; proxy fixes for compressed replies and IPv6 shadowing; charts that draw themselves in, totals with a fortnight of history and a move-since-yesterday chip, a ninety-day rhythm strip, a seven-day mean on the trend, dashboard pages in the island's own type and texture, and the window opens on the display you are using. 0.8.0 (Sep 5, 2026): computer use — the agent you already run can drive macOS apps, and every GUI action passes your approval gate first, with readable approvals and a live feed of what it did; a Skills page showing every skill advertised to every prompt, what it costs you and which ones were ever used, with a bulk switch for the dead weight; a Context budget page measuring what a session weighs before you type, broken down into skills, subagents, CLAUDE.md and each MCP server's tool schemas — switch a server off and its schemas leave the prompt; sessions now show what they were carrying at hello. 0.7.0: reply to Claude from your phone, Bark push, plan review with feedback, safe Allow-all, focus-aware surfacing, tmux/WezTerm/kitty precision, more agents, calibrated forecast, SSH Remote, Homebrew, Monthly Wrapped, display currency, iCloud settings sync.

How do approvals work?

A tiny hook (installed by the first-run wizard, with your settings.json backed up first) forwards Claude Code permission requests to the notch. If TokenLens isn't running, your normal terminal prompt takes over — it can never block or silently allow anything.

What does the trial include?

Everything — for 14 days, no card. After that the app keeps running; you'll just see a gentle reminder to activate. No features are ripped out from under you.

Can I move my license to a new Mac?

Yes. Deactivate in Settings ▸ License on the old Mac and activate on the new one. A 1-Mac license means one Mac at a time.
